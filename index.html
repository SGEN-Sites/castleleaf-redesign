What do I need to know about being an Adult-Use (recreational) customer?

For starters, CastleLeaf is an Adult-Use only retail provider. That said, we strive to educate adult-use consumers about the therapeutic benefits of cannabis.

Adult use (recreational) consumers can shop at any adult-use dispensary with a valid government ID that shows they are 21+, can purchase up to 1oz per day, and edibles are capped at 5mg per serving and 100mg per package.

I am from out of state. Can I make a purchase at CastleLeaf?

Yes! Anyone 21+ with a valid government ID can make a purchase including out of state.
