Cannabis Concentrates

Extracts such as shatter and wax, with a higher level of THC than flower, so a small amount is plenty.

Shop ConcentratesAll categories
01

What it is

Concentrates are extracts that draw the cannabinoids and aromas out of the plant into a smaller, stronger form. They come in many textures, from glassy shatter to soft wax, and they're enjoyed with dedicated gear.

02

How people choose

Method comes first: some people use a dab rig, others a concentrate vaporizer. Then come the texture, the strain and the extraction style. Massachusetts allows no more than 5 grams of concentrates per day, so it pays to choose thoughtfully.

03

What to ask a budtender

Ask which gear a concentrate needs, how much to use for a first try and how to store it. If you're new to cannabis, ask about a gentler format first.

Budtender tip

With concentrates, think crumb, not spoonful. Start tiny and you'll never regret it.

Questions

Concentrates, answered

Are concentrates stronger than flower?
Yes. Concentrates have a higher level of THC than flower, which is why they're best for experienced consumers.
How much concentrate can I buy?
Massachusetts allows no more than 5 grams of concentrates per day. Our cannabis laws guide explains how concentrates count toward the daily limit.
