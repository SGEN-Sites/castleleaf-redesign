Edibles: Start Low, Go Slow

Cannabis 1014 min read

The golden rule of edibles fits on a sticky note: start low, go slow. Here’s why it matters and how to plan a first experience you’ll actually enjoy.

In this article

  1. The golden rule
  2. Why edibles are different
  3. Planning a first try
  4. A few good habits
  5. If you take too much
  6. Questions and answers

The golden rule

If you remember one thing about edibles, make it this: start low, go slow. We said it in How Does Cannabis Work?, where we suggested 2.5 mg to 5 mg for people new to edibles, and we’ll happily keep saying it. It’s the difference between a lovely evening and a very long one.

Cannabis is for everyone, and so is taking your time with it. Here’s why edibles deserve a little extra patience.

Why edibles are different

When you inhale cannabis, it reaches your bloodstream through your lungs and you notice the effects fairly quickly. When you eat it, it takes the scenic route: through your stomach and then your liver, which converts part of the THC into a cousin called 11-hydroxy-THC. Many people find the result feels different from inhaling, and often stronger.

That journey explains two things every edible fan should know:

  • They take longer. Effects can begin anywhere from about half an hour to two hours after eating, sometimes longer, depending on your body and what else is in your stomach.
  • They last longer. An edible’s effects tend to stretch over several hours, often longer than people expect.

The classic mistake is to feel nothing after an hour, decide it isn’t working and take another piece. Then both arrive at once.

Planning a first try

  1. 1

    Read the label

    Check the milligrams of THC per serving. In Massachusetts, edibles are capped at 5 mg of THC a serving and 100 mg a package.

  2. 2

    Choose a small dose

    Start at or below one serving. Many edibles can be cut, so half a serving is a perfectly good place to begin.

  3. 3

    Pick your setting

    Somewhere comfortable and private, with nowhere to be and no driving. A familiar room and good company make a difference.

  4. 4

    Wait, then wait some more

    Give it at least two hours before you even think about more. A kitchen timer is a wonderful thing.

  5. 5

    Make a note

    Write down the product, the dose and what you noticed. Next time, you’ll know exactly where to start.

Tinctures are measured with a dropper rather than by the piece, and the same rule applies: a small amount, then patience.

A few good habits

  • Skip the alcohol. Mixing the two makes the experience harder to predict.
  • Keep edibles in their original packaging, stored away from anyone under 21 and from pets. Many look just like ordinary snacks.
  • Enjoy them on private property only. Public consumption is illegal in Massachusetts, and driving under the influence is illegal and dangerous.
  • If you take medication or have a health condition, talk to a doctor first.

If you take too much

It happens, and it passes. Find a quiet, comfortable spot, sip some water, put on something familiar and remind yourself that the feeling is temporary. It’s nice to have a trusted friend nearby. If you feel unwell or worried, contact a medical professional, and in an emergency, call 911.

New to edibles? Ask one of our budtenders to walk you through serving sizes and flavors before you choose. Browse edibles and tinctures anytime, then pick up in store or order local delivery.

Questions and answers

How long before I feel an edible?
Anywhere from about half an hour to two hours, sometimes longer. It depends on your body and what you’ve eaten, so give it a full two hours before you take more.
What’s a good first dose?
A small one. In How Does Cannabis Work? we suggested 2.5 mg to 5 mg for people new to edibles. Everyone responds differently, so the low end is a fine place to start.
Why do edibles feel different from smoking?
When you eat cannabis, your liver processes the THC and turns some of it into a related compound, 11-hydroxy-THC. Many people find the experience stronger and longer lasting as a result.
