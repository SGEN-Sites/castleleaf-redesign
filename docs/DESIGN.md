# Design: "Lantern Keep"

CastleLeaf's store is a white brick building with two round towers, its mark is a crown, and its team plans an in-house
gallery for local artists. The concept is that keep by lantern light, built from four ideas:

- **Ethereal:** luminous linen and cream halls with soft pools of sage and copper light, a fine grain and generous space.
- **Tenebrism:** a few gallery-dark stages where one subject is lit by a single warm beam against deep darkness.
- **Glassmorphism:** frosted panels that hold copy and controls over varied backgrounds, with a light rim that follows the pointer.
- **Bento:** sections arranged as grids of tiles of different sizes with one gap and one radius, so each reads at a glance.

Depth comes from frames that are anchored and break their bounds: an arched frame (the shape of the towers' windows) that
stands in one section and rises into the next, and product cut-outs that stand on a tile and rise above its edge. Nothing floats.

**Brand kept:** CastleLeaf's palette (sage #719370, cream #F7EDE1, charcoal #2D2A27, navy #242B43, copper #B87962), its logo
and crown, its building, its words and its tone. The type is Fraunces for headings and Figtree for text, free stand-ins for
the brand's licensed faces.

**Motion:** scroll reveals, a scroll-driven hero, a horizontal category track, parallax on the breaking frames, hover lifts,
a lantern light on the dark stages and a light sweep on the footer wordmark. All of it is switched off for visitors who ask
their system for reduced motion.
