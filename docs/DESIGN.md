# Design: "Lantern Keep" in obsidian and gold

CastleLeaf's store is a white brick building with two round towers, its mark is a crown, and its current deal art is
black stone brushed with metallic gold paint. The concept is that keep by lantern light, in that black and gold, built
from four ideas:

- **Ethereal:** soft golden haze and fine gold dust drifting over black, gentle pools of warm light, a fine grain and
  generous space.
- **Tenebrism:** black stages where one subject is lit by a single warm golden beam.
- **Glassmorphism:** smoked black glass panels with a fine gold rim hold copy and controls over textured grounds, with a
  soft light that follows the pointer.
- **Bento:** sections arranged as grids of tiles of different sizes with one gap and one radius, so each reads at a glance.

**Obsidian and gold paint.** Generated textures carry the material: glossy liquid obsidian with gold flecks, black stone
crossed by gold veins, a broad stroke and two long ribbons of metallic gold paint, gold leaf and gold haze. Bands use the
stone under a dark veil so text stays calm; the gold paint is blended in with `screen`, so its black ground falls away.
Gold leaf fills one band at most on a page. Main buttons are metallic gold with dark text.

**Frames.** Every frame, card and tile is a rounded rectangle; there are no arches or domes. Depth comes from frames that
are anchored and break their bounds: a frame that stands in one section and rises into the next, and product cut-outs
that stand on a tile and rise above its edge. Nothing floats.

**Brand kept:** CastleLeaf's black and gold, sampled from the client's own deal art (black #10100F; gold #9E711D,
#B4852A, #D5A43D), its cream #F7EDE1 as the ivory text colour, its logo (shown in ivory on black) and crown, its building,
its words and its tone. On the black grounds, body text is ivory at 10:1 or more, gold words use the bright gold at 6.6:1
or more, and button text on gold is 5.9:1 or more. The type is Fraunces for headings and Figtree for text, free stand-ins for the brand's licensed
faces.

**Motion:** scroll reveals that keep their rounded corners, a scroll-driven hero, a horizontal category track, parallax on
the breaking frames, hover lifts, a lantern light on the dark stages and a gold light sweep on the footer wordmark. All of
it is switched off for visitors who ask their system for reduced motion.
