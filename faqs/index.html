What do I need to know about being an Adult-Use (recreational) customer?

For starters, CastleLeaf is an Adult-Use only retail provider. That said, we strive to educate adult-use consumers about the therapeutic benefits of cannabis.

Adult use (recreational) consumers can shop at any adult-use dispensary with a valid government ID that shows they are 21+, can purchase up to 1oz per day, and edibles are capped at 5mg per serving and 100mg per package.

What will be for sale?

We carry a range of the highest quality cannabis products available in Massachusetts, including dry flower, pre-rolled joints, edibles, vape pens, concentrates, topicals, tinctures, and more.

How much can I purchase at once?

MA state law allows customers to purchase up to 1oz (28 grams) flower equivalent per day and no more than 5 grams of concentrates. You can buy a mix of products including; flower, edibles, and concentrates.

  • Because some products (like concentrates) have a higher level of THC than flower, you won’t be able to purchase the same amount in both forms. The ‘Marijuana Equivalency’ rules equate 1oz of retail (recreational) flower = 8g of concentrate (shatter, wax, etc.) or 800mg of active THC in an infused food or beverage
  • It’s important to remember that these regulations apply to sales, not possession. As per Massachusetts State Law, you are allowed to legally possess 1oz (or 28g) of THC at any one time.

What do I need to make a purchase? Form of ID?

Anyone 21+ with a valid government issued ID can make a purchase at CastleLeaf.

I am from out of state. Can I make a purchase at CastleLeaf?

Yes! Anyone 21+ with a valid government ID can make a purchase including out of state.

What form of payment do you accept?

We accept cash or debit cards. If you forget cash, no worries…you’ve got you covered with an ATM on site.

Can I drive with cannabis in my car?

Yes, as long as you are transporting your purchase in a sealed container only. Consuming while driving is illegal and dangerous.

Can I travel out of Massachusetts with Cannabis?

Cannabis is not legal at the federal level in the United States, so travel outside of the state of Massachusetts with cannabis products is prohibited.

Can I consume anywhere?

Public consumption is illegal in MA. We ask that you respect the law and the community that we operate in. Please consume responsibly on private property only.

Is there parking at your dispensary?

Yes! We have plenty of on site parking in our parking lot as well as street parking in front of the dispensary.

What is your return policy?

All sales are final. In the unlikely event you receive a defective product we will happily replace it for you at a manager’s discretion. For any return questions, email us at: info@castleleaf.com

Do you offer discounts?

Due to Massachusetts state regulations we cannot offer discounts on recreational cannabis. We do offer product bundles and competitive everyday low prices.
