Your Neighborhood Dispensary

Welcome to Castleleaf Dispensary & Craft Kitchen, your trusted neighborhood destination for premium cannabis products. We’re proud to serve our local community with carefully curated cannabis products, knowledgeable staff, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
At Castleleaf, quality and consistency comes first. Whether you’re seasoned enthusiast or new to cannabis, our team is here to help You find the right flower, concentrates, edibles, or wellness products to fit your lifestyle.
