Delivery questions

All FAQs

Who can receive a delivery?
An adult 21 or older with a valid government ID. The simplest plan is to be home yourself, with your ID ready, when the order arrives.
Can I order for pickup instead?
Yes. When you order online you can choose pick-up in-store or Home Delivery. Order from home and swing by when you're ready: we're at 100 Leo M Birmingham Pkwy in Brighton.
Can I talk to a budtender first?
Please do. Give us a call at (617) 202-5066 or stop by and chat with one of our budtenders. Booking a budtender online is coming soon on our Book A Budtender page.
What do I need to make a purchase? Form of ID?

Anyone 21+ with a valid government issued ID can make a purchase at CastleLeaf.

I am from out of state. Can I make a purchase at CastleLeaf?

Yes! Anyone 21+ with a valid government ID can make a purchase including out of state.
